About

I care less about how much a product does, and more about whether it holds up — tested, secure, and light enough to load instantly.

My focus is software engineering first, then AI, cloud and DevOps. InternTrack.ai came out of a problem I had myself: applications scattered across spreadsheets and inboxes, with no honest view of where I stood. It now tracks applications, interviews and offers, analyses resumes against job descriptions, and flags skill gaps.

The same thinking runs through my other work — a face-recognition attendance system that removes proxy-prone roll calls, and smaller tools built to be finished rather than demoed.

At university I'm Student Coordinator of the Developer's Club and an organiser for SutraTech-2K25. I took part in Smart India Hackathon 2025 and the Google Cloud Arcade Facilitator program.

My engineering is funded by the Cummins Nurturing Brilliance Scholarship, which selected me from roughly 12,000 applicants — the fact I'm proudest of on this page, and the standard I try to work to.
